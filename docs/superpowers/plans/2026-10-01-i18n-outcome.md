# i18n: outcome (2026-10-03)

The record of how the plan `2026-10-01-i18n.md` was carried out: the decisions that departed from it, how the result was checked, and what was found along the way to fix separately. The working ledger it comes from was deleted with the plan's workspace.

## What shipped

Tools for Autodarts speaks English, German and Dutch. It follows the language set on autodarts (`localStorage["autodarts.settings.language"]`, written by Settings → General and the user menu). It has no setting of its own, and it switches at once when the user picks a language on the page.
- **Translated:** every feature, its settings, everything drawn into the site's pages, notifications and the Discord announcement (in the host's language).
- **The note:** `/settings/general` says, under the Language setting, that it changes Tools too.
- **The core:** a small in-repo module (`utils/i18n/`, `components/AppTrans.vue`, `composables/useI18n.ts`), with catalogs in `locales/{en,de,nl}` and the site's own words in `locales/GLOSSARY.md`.
- **Guards for new text:**
  - `yarn i18n:check`, for the catalogs and the code. Besides template text and DOM writes, it also flags:
    - prose a function returns;
    - prose `ref()` starts with;
    - prose assigned to a ref's `.value`;
    - prose an `&&` or a `||`/`??` default gives in those places;
    - text drawn through a `data-adt-<name>-label` or `-message` attribute;
    - a German or Dutch word copied from the English, unless `locales/same-as-english.json` lists it (103 German and 94 Dutch keys today).

    What it still can't see is listed in CLAUDE.md: a single word a function returns, text kept in a variable or an object property (fallbacks included) and shown by name, and text handed to a function of your own. Setting `sessionStorage["autodarts.i18n.debug"] = "true"` finds those;
  - `yarn i18n:site`, for the site's own wording;
  - the ESLint rule `vue/no-bare-strings-in-template`;
  - `locales/untranslated.json`;
  - the "Translations" section of CLAUDE.md, under which everything new ships in all three languages.

## Found along the way: other bugs, not caused by this work

- **WLED with WLED switched off:** leaving any lobby or match sends the IDLE effect anyway. `wledFxOnRemove()` runs `setEffectByTrigger("idle")`, and both lobby.content (on every non-lobby page) and match.content (on leaving a match) call it unconditionally. Pre-existing since the feature was added in July 2025.
- **Feature cards at 390 px:** six of them clip their On/Off toggle in every language, because the card has a fixed height: Autostart, Recent Local Players, Local Lobby, Teams, QR Code and Quick Correction.
- **iOS 15.0–15.3** lack `Array.prototype.at`, and the app targets iOS 15.0. It is used in `utils/win.ts:24`, `entrypoints/content/v2-menu.ts:49`, `entrypoints/lobby.content/teams.ts:662` and `entrypoints/match.content/instant-replay.ts:595`.
- **`DART_LANDED_LABELS`** lacks the site's German "Dart geworfen" and Dutch "Dart geland". The Quiet Own Darts row is still placed, by its structural fallback (checked live), but the comment above the list still says the site ships only English.
- **`ConfirmDialog`'s icon-only close button** has no accessible name.
- **The DOM picker's index generator** misses every generic call, such as `querySelector<HTMLElement>(…)`: 16 at 480ade6 and 25 now. The fix is `\s*(?:<[^>()]*>)?\s*\(` in `scripts/build-selector-index.mjs`, then `yarn picker:index`.
- **`yarn dev`'s reload can miss the browser:** Chrome kept serving a content script from before a rebuild until the extension was reloaded in place over CDP.

## The check's known limits

CLAUDE.md lists the main ones. The last re-review added detail:
- **The `.value` rule skips receivers named like elements** (`input`, `target`, `field`, `*El`), plus `+=`/`&&=` and single words. CLAUDE.md doesn't spell this out.
- **`prose()` reads `name(` as code**, and its comment's list of limits leaves that out.
- **A sentence of two clauses, the first capitalised and joined by `;`**, passes unseen. Example: "Note: it is saved; tip: use the board."
- **A stale comment:** `entrypoints/match.content/QuickCorrection.vue:118` names only MISS and BULL as untranslated; `25` is too.
