# 🎯 Tools for Autodarts v3.0.7 🎯

### 🚨 Sound works again on Firefox and Safari
**Sound FX, the Caller and WLED never fired during a match** on those two browsers in 3.0.4. If you are on either, this is the update you want *(#238)*.

### 🔊 Sound
⏱️ **A long sound no longer holds up the ones behind it.** The 17-second gameshot a stock setup ships kept every dart of the next visits waiting, then played them all at once. The wait between a dart landing and its sound went from 1.7 seconds to four milliseconds.

🔇 **Quiet Own Darts** silences your own darts on Firefox, where it had no effect at all, and for a guest you added in the lobby.

### 🖥️ Match screen
📊 **Larger Legs / Sets**, **Larger Player Names**, **Larger Player Match Data** and **Smaller Scores** work at every window size — all four only ever did anything on the widest layout.

📹 **Board View** stays on the view you picked, in place of going back to camera 1 on a bot's turn.

🗂️ A second Autodarts tab no longer takes every match feature down with it.

### 🔍 Darts Zoom
🎯 Each close-up shows **its own dart**, in the middle of the tile, in place of a frame from a visit or two back. **Top** is a proper strip across the board now, and the bull-off is left alone.

### ✨ New
🖐️ **Put the action bar where you want it.** Take hold of Autodarts' own undo and Next buttons and move them anywhere; *Reset bar position* in the Darts Zoom settings puts them back.

### 🤝 Community
Thanks to **@tobias973** *(#238)* for the report behind the Firefox and Safari fix, and to all of you in the bug channels for the rest 🎉
